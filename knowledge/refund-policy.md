Customers may return an item within 30 days of purchase, provided they have a
receipt.[^owner-interview-2026-09-21]

Once a return is accepted, the refund lands within 5 working days.[^owner-interview-2026-09-21]

## Conditions

Returned items must be unused.[^owner-interview-2026-09-21]

## Exceptions

Faulty goods are exempt from the 30-day return window.[^owner-interview-2026-09-21]
